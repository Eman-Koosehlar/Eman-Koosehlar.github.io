08 · SCIENTIFIC NOTES
What a real pipeline adds

Somatic variant calling and annotation (e.g. VEP), clonality and purity estimation, allele-specific expression, HLA typing, peptide processing and binding prediction (e.g. NetMHCpan, MHCflurry via pVACtools), self-similarity checks, and expert review.

Why VAF and TPM?

Higher VAF suggests a variant is present in more tumor cells (more clonal); expression evidence suggests the mutant transcript could actually be translated and presented.

Why HLA matters

Each HLA allele presents peptides with particular anchor residues (usually positions 2 and 9 for 9-mers), so the same variant can be a good candidate for one patient and irrelevant for another.

Why a conceptual construct?

Real designs need codon and UTR optimization, nucleoside modification choices, delivery formulation, and extensive safety and immunogenicity testing — none of which is attempted here.
