    08

    Method & limitations

    Synthetic by design

    All profiles are hand-written teaching examples. No sequencing data, patient data or experimental results are shown or implied.

    Site-specific logic

    Gut, vaginal and oral ecosystems follow different rules — e.g. low diversity is typical in the vagina but not the gut — so each site uses its own components.

    What it cannot do

    No strain-level resolution, absolute abundance, metabolomics, host data, or validated cut-offs. Microbiome interpretation is highly context-dependent.
